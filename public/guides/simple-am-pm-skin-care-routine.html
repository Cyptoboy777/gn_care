Routine guide

A simple AM and PM skin care routine

A routine you follow every day with three or four steps beats a long shelf you give up on in a week.

Start with the core three

A gentle cleanser, a moisturizer and a sunscreen cover almost everything most people need. Get these working before adding anything else.

Morning

  1. Cleanse with a gentle cleanser. If your skin is dry or sensitive, rinsing with water is enough.
  2. Optional serum such as vitamin C, applied to clean, dry skin.
  3. Moisturize with a texture that suits your skin type.
  4. Sunscreen last, SPF 30 or higher and broad spectrum.

Night

  1. Remove sunscreen and dirt. If you wore sunscreen or makeup, use an oil or balm cleanser first, then your regular cleanser.
  2. Treatment such as a retinoid or an exfoliating acid, on dry skin and not every night at the start.
  3. Moisturize to support the skin barrier.

Order of application

Go from the thinnest texture to the thickest, and water-based products before oil-based ones. Sunscreen is always the final step in the morning.

Add products slowly

  • Introduce one new product at a time and wait about two weeks before the next.
  • Patch test on the jaw or behind the ear for three days.
  • Mild tingling can happen with actives. Burning, swelling or lasting redness means stop.

Match textures to your skin

  • Oily: gel cleanser and oil-free gel moisturizer.
  • Dry: cream cleanser, hydrating serum and a richer cream.
  • Combination: lighter lotion on the T-zone, richer cream on the cheeks.
  • Sensitive: fragrance-free products and fewer steps.
This guide is general information, not medical advice. See a dermatologist for acne, eczema or other skin conditions.
