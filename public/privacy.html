Privacy Policy

GN Care does not ask you to create an account, and the notes, routines and photos you add stay on your device.

Who we are

GN Personal Hygiene and Skin Care ("GN Care") is a website and web app at https://gncare.xyz, run by cryptoboy. You can reach us at theh386@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your routine steps, water log, skin check-ins, shelf and budget entries, face scan notes, flags and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. We never receive them. If you add a selfie on the Face Scan screen, it is processed only in your browser to estimate shine, redness, evenness and under-eye darkness. It is never uploaded. It stays in this session unless you choose "Save photo on this device", which stores a compressed copy in your browser only. Clearing your browser data, or using Settings > Reset all data, removes everything.

Shopping links

The Shop tab and Face Scan kit link to Amazon.in and Flipkart. If you buy through those links, GN Care may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate and Flipkart affiliate, GN Care earns from qualifying purchases. Amazon or Flipkart may set their own cookies once you open their sites. Your skin type, budget and shelf stay on your device and are not sent to Amazon or Flipkart. If you tap "Explain my kit with AI", the skin type, budget and chosen product names are sent to our AI provider to write the answer.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google Consent Mode v2. Until you choose, ad and analytics storage is denied. A banner offers Accept, Reject or Customise (necessary cookies, ads personalisation, and analytics). Necessary cookies always stay on so the app and your choice can be saved. If you accept, ads may be personalised. If you reject, we load Google AdSense only for non-personalised ads. AdSense is not loaded until you make a choice, and not at all until a publisher ID is set in the site config. You can change your choice later in Settings > Cookies and ads. We do not load an analytics script unless you opt in and we later add one.

  • Manage Google's ad personalisation: adssettings.google.com
  • How Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

AI Coach and label decoder

The Coach screen works in three ways. The label scan runs on your device and sends nothing. If you switch on "Run AI on my device", an open-source AI model is downloaded to your browser and answers there, so your question stays on your device. Otherwise, when you press "Ask" or "Explain with AI", the text you typed is sent through our server to an AI provider and the answer is shown to you. If you switch on "Use my skin profile", your skin type, sensitivity and sun exposure level are sent too. Nothing else about you is sent.

  • Please do not type your name, contact details or medical history.
  • Our default provider is Cloudflare Workers AI. Cloudflare states that it does not use customer content to train AI models and does not keep inputs or outputs after the request is processed. See Cloudflare's data usage page. If we change provider, we will update this page.
  • The on-device model files are downloaded from public hosts (a CDN, Hugging Face and GitHub), which can see your IP address during the download.
  • AI features are for people aged 18 and over, and may be unavailable in some regions.
  • To limit misuse and cost, we keep temporary counters based on a one-way hash of your IP address (not the address itself), and we may keep an answer for up to 24 hours so that repeated questions are not sent again.

Ratings and feedback

If you send a rating or feedback, we receive the star rating, the type you chose, your message (up to 500 characters) and the app version. We store it in a private Google Sheet to improve the app. We do not collect your name or email unless you type them in the message, so please do not. When you tap "Yes" or "No" under an AI answer, only the thumb rating is sent, not your question.

Other services

  • Hosting: the site and its small server functions run on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI (or another AI provider) and Google Sheets: used for the online AI Coach and for feedback, as described above.
  • Upstash (optional): if enabled, stores the temporary counters and cached answers described above.
  • Fonts: fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

Notifications

If you set a routine, we may ask for permission to show reminders on this device for your morning and night steps and for sunscreen reapply. These are local notifications scheduled in your browser or shown when you open the app. We do not use a paid push service, and we do not send notifications from our servers.

Children

GN Care is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can clear stored data in your browser, change your ad choice in Settings, block cookies in your browser, or leave the site at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Questions can be sent to theh386@gmail.com.
