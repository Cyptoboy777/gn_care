About

A calmer way to look after your skin

GN Care is a free skin care companion. It helps you keep a morning and night routine, remember sunscreen and water, plan a monthly budget in rupees, and check whether your products suit each other.

What is inside

  • Morning and night routine tracker with a streak
  • UV guidance and a sunscreen reapply countdown
  • Face scan in four quick steps: an optional selfie, a short note, your budget, then a kit and a one-tap AM/PM routine (cosmetic estimate, not a medical diagnosis)
  • Ritual timer for masks, cleansers and hair oil
  • Shelf and budget tracker with product expiry dates
  • Label decoder that reads an ingredient list on your device
  • Ingredient checker for common active pairings
  • AI coach for general skin and hygiene questions

How it is funded

The app is free to use and is supported by advertising and affiliate links, which we label clearly. Ads never change what the checker or the guides say. AI answers are general information and can be wrong. Shop and kit links go to Amazon.in and Flipkart. As an Amazon Associate and Flipkart affiliate, GN Care earns from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you.
